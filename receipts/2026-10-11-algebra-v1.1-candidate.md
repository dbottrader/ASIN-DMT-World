## Defects in v1.0 (confirmed)

1. `tau_hard = (963/428) * tau_soft * (2/3) = 1.85` is false twice over:
   - The expression evaluates exactly to 21/20 = 1.05, not 1.85.
   - 1.05 is also wrong as a gate threshold: 963/428 is a frequency ratio
     (Hz/Hz); Delta-717 is EMA(1 − R) on [0,1]. Comparing them is a
     category error. (§2 calling 2.250 the "hard threshold" is the same error.)
2. With tau_soft = 0.70 and Δ ∈ [0,1], AMBER (`0.70 > Δ ≥ tau_hard`) is
   unsatisfiable for tau_hard = 1.85 or 1.05, and VETO (`Δ < tau_hard`)
   covers the whole domain.

## Repair (minimal, no invention)

- Keep `963/428 = 9/4` as a frequency ratio only.
- Keep `tau_soft = 0.70`.
- Keep the three FCG inequalities exactly as written (v1.0 §6, lines 269–271).
- Replace the tau_hard definition: **tau_hard = 0.25** — the only floor in
  the document that sits in [0,1] and below tau_soft (v1.0 §4, line 119:
  "Delta-717 < 0.25 → CP8 VETO").
- Delete both `= 1.85` and `(963/428) * tau_soft * (2/3)` from tau_hard's
  definition. No rearrangement of 9/4, 2/3, 0.70 yields 0.25.
